A ten-to-one countdown of Microsoft 365 Copilot features people already have but often miss. Every demo happens inside April's Analog, a fictional record shop preparing for a three-night music festival.

Setup at a glance

  1. Create the Analog Fest SharePoint site and upload the supplied plan, recap, and notes.
  2. Upload the inventory, weekend sales, chart, writing samples, and skills to OneDrive.
  3. Seed Outlook with the supplied PowerShell script or import the individual EML files.
  4. Create the Teams meeting, Confluence Stage Ops space, and Jira SHOP project.
  5. Enable the required Copilot features, connect the data sources, and build the Analog Fest Notebook.
  6. Rehearse with the night-before and morning-of checklists in the included setup guide.

What the sample data is designed to reveal

  • The inventory has 2,000 records and plenty of stock older than 90 days.
  • Jazz and Blues sales are down week over week, while Soul spikes during a DJ night.
  • The festival documents intentionally disagree so Copilot has changes and conflicts to find.
  • The Jira project contains a high-priority checkout bug that threatens weekend-pass sales.
  • The Confluence pages contain the stage checklist, load-in rules, band deal, and stage plot.
