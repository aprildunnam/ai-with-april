Vibe session · Copilot Cowork

Delegating Real Work to Copilot Cowork

Stop prompting and start handing off. This session shows where Cowork fits in the Copilot family, how it plans cross-app work, and how to turn one successful task into an app, automation, skill, or plugin.

Download the slide deck (.pptx) Download the demo pack (.zip) Open the prompt sheet (.txt)

What's in the 436 KB download: the setup guide, copy-paste prompts, seeded Outlook data, a Teams chat script, vendor and festival files, SharePoint list CSVs, and fallback artifacts. The optimized 6.6 MB slide deck is available directly above. The large demo recordings remain prepared for distribution through GitHub Releases.

Prompts to try first

Clear the festival inbox

Go through my unread email from this week. Flag anything about
Analog Fest that needs me, sort it into a short list by urgency,
and draft replies to the quick ones. Show me the drafts before
sending anything.

Prepare the vendor meeting

Prep me for tomorrow's 10am with Crate & Needle Records.
Pull our last 6 months of email with them, the pop-up request,
any Teams chats about them, and their past orders from
Vendor_Pipeline.xlsx. Build a one-page Word briefing with
3 talking points and save it to /Analog Fest/Meetings/.
Preview before you create anything.

Build the check-in app

/app Build an Analog Fest volunteer check-in app. Volunteers pick
their name and shift and tap Check in. The shift lead sees who's
checked in and who's missing for the current shift. Use the
Volunteers list in the Analog Fest SharePoint site. Keep it simple
and mobile-friendly.

The delegation model

PartWhat to specify
GoalThe outcome you want, not every click.
SourcesThe files, people, systems, or date range it should use.
FormatThe length, structure, tone, and destination.
BoundaryWhat it may do, what it must preview, and when it should ask.

Setup at a glance

  1. Seed the demo mailbox with the supplied PowerShell script and keep the unread messages current.
  2. Post the supplied messages in a one-to-one Teams chat.
  3. Upload the vendor pipeline and festival plan, then create the meetings folder.
  4. Create the Volunteers and Pop-up SharePoint lists from the supplied CSV files.
  5. Enable Cowork, Copilot Credits, /app, and the Miro plugin or monday.com backup.
  6. Build the volunteer app once in rehearsal and follow the reset checklist before presenting.

What the sample data is designed to reveal

  • The unread inbox separates urgent approvals, quick replies, and low-value noise.
  • Crate & Needle has a six-month relationship, two orders, one late shipment, and a pending pop-up request.
  • Tape Hiss Records ordered recently enough to qualify for an automatic “yes” response.
  • The volunteer list has 40 people across six shifts, with Sunday late intentionally understaffed.
  • The fallback skill demonstrates the same open SKILL.md format used across Cowork, PowerPoint, Excel, and Agent Builder.
