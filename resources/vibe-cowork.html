Brief it like a teammate

Don't describe every click. Specify the outcome, the evidence to use, and where Cowork must stop for you.

PartWhat to specify
GoalThe outcome you want, in one line.
SourcesThe files, people, systems, or date range it should use.
FormatThe length, structure, tone, and destination.
BoundaryWhat it may do, what it must preview, and when it should ask.

When to delegate, and what to review

Use Cowork for multi-step, cross-app work. Keep a one-line edit in the app you're already using; use a fixed workflow for a fixed rule. Think through the brief in Chat, hand the outcome to Cowork, then polish the result in Word or Excel.

Read the plan, inspect action previews, interrupt when the scope changes, and treat outputs as drafts. Be deliberate about granting permission to skip future approval prompts. Ambiguous requests, stale source data, and long dependency chains still need your judgment.

What IT needs to check

The slides also cover the runtime boundary, model choices, Microsoft Purview, access and spending policies, plugins, and usage. Review your tenant's settings and the current documentation before opening access. Skills do not grant permissions of their own.

Compare the cost of a task with the time it actually saves. The deck's cost examples are illustrative, not a pricing guarantee; check your current rate and usage.
