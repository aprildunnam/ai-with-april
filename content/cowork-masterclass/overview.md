# What is Microsoft Copilot Cowork?

## Overview

**Microsoft Copilot** is an AI-powered assistant built into Microsoft 365 that works alongside you in the tools you use every day — Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, and more. It is powered by large language models (LLMs) combined with your organization's data through the Microsoft Graph.

**Copilot Cowork** refers to the collaborative, AI-assisted way of working that Copilot enables. Rather than replacing human effort, Copilot acts as a co-worker — handling repetitive or time-consuming tasks so you can focus on higher-value work.

---

## Why Does It Matter?

- ⏱️ **Save Time** — Automate drafting, summarizing, and reformatting content.
- 🤝 **Collaborate Better** — Get meeting summaries, action items, and follow-up drafts instantly in Teams.
- 💡 **Unlock Insights** — Use natural language to analyze data in Excel or query information across your organization.
- 📝 **Communicate Clearly** — Let Copilot help you draft, edit, and refine emails and documents.

---

## Where Does Copilot Live?

Copilot is embedded across the Microsoft 365 ecosystem:

| App | What Copilot Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Word** | Draft, rewrite, summarize, and improve documents |
| **Excel** | Analyze data, suggest formulas, and create charts from natural language |
| **PowerPoint** | Generate presentations from a prompt or a Word document |
| **Outlook** | Draft emails, summarize threads, and suggest replies |
| **Teams** | Summarize meetings, capture action items, answer questions about the conversation |
| **OneNote** | Organize and summarize notes |
| **Loop** | Collaborate in real time with AI-enhanced components |
| **Microsoft 365 Chat** | Ask questions across all your Microsoft 365 data |

## Key Concepts

### Grounding
Copilot uses **grounding** to connect LLM responses to your organization's real data via Microsoft Graph. This means answers are based on your emails, documents, meetings, and chats — not just public internet data.

### Prompting
The quality of Copilot's output depends heavily on the quality of your **prompts**. A good prompt is:
- **Specific** — clearly defines the task
- **Contextual** — gives Copilot relevant background
- **Directed** — specifies the format or tone you want

### Iteration
Copilot is designed for iteration. Treat the first response as a starting point and refine it using follow-up prompts.

## Responsible AI

Microsoft Copilot is built on Microsoft's **Responsible AI principles**:

- Fairness
- Reliability & Safety
- Privacy & Security
- Inclusiveness
- Transparency
- Accountability

Copilot respects your organization's existing permissions — it only surfaces content that the user already has access to.
