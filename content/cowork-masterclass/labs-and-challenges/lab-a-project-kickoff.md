# Lab A — The New Project Kickoff

**Time:** ~25 min · **Team:** 3–4 · **Files:** `01_ProjectAtlas_Brief.md`, `02_ProjectAtlas_KickoffEmail.txt`

## Scenario
You've just been handed a new cross-functional project. Use Cowork to go from a pile of context to *organized and ready to lead* — fast.

## Your mission
1. Upload the brief and kickoff email.
2. Get the lay of the land:
   ```
   Read the Project Atlas brief and kickoff email. Give me a one-page summary: objective, scope (in/out), timeline, team, and the top 3 risks.
   ```
3. Turn it into action:
   ```
   Draft a 30-minute kickoff meeting agenda that closes the two open decisions, and a stakeholder email inviting the team with what to bring.
   ```
4. Pressure-test it:
   ```
   What's missing or unclear in this plan? List the questions I should resolve before we start.
   ```

## Deliverable
A one-page project summary **+** a ready-to-send kickoff email and agenda.

## Share-out (3 min)
- What was your approach?
- What did Cowork catch that you might have missed?
- Where would this save you the most time?

## Stretch
`Set up a scheduled task to compile a Project Atlas status every Friday at 4pm.`
