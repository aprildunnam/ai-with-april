# Lab C — The Inbox Zero Sprint

**Time:** ~25 min · **Team:** 3–4 · **Files:** `06_Inbox_Samples.txt` (or your own inbox)

## Scenario
Your inbox is out of control. Regain order — and draft your way back to zero.

## Your mission
1. Upload `06_Inbox_Samples.txt` (or point Cowork at your real inbox).
2. Triage:
   ```
   Triage these emails by priority. Separate "needs action from me" from newsletters/noise, and tell me what's time-sensitive.
   ```
3. Clear the deck:
   ```
   Draft short, appropriate replies to the action-needed items. Flag anything that needs a decision rather than a reply.
   ```
4. Close the loop:
   ```
   Summarize what's left and suggest what I can archive or unsubscribe from.
   ```

## Deliverable
A triaged inbox plan **+** 2–3 drafted replies.

## Share-out (3 min)
- What did it correctly flag as noise?
- Would you let it draft replies unattended? Where's your comfort line?

## Stretch
`Create a skill called "inbox triage" that does this every morning and shows me only what needs me.`
