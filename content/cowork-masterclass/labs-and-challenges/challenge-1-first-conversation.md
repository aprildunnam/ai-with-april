# Challenge 1 — Your First Cowork Conversation

**Time:** ~8 min · **You'll need:** nothing (your own inbox & calendar)

## Goal
Feel the difference between a single-shot prompt and a multi-step, cross-app conversation with an AI *coworker*.

## Steps
1. Open **Copilot Cowork**. Leave the model on **Auto**.
2. Pick **one** task below and type it exactly (swap in a real name/doc):

   ```
   What's on my calendar today and what should I prioritize?
   ```
   ```
   Summarize the last 5 emails from [person] and tell me what needs my attention.
   ```
   ```
   Find the latest version of [document] and give me the key points.
   ```
3. **Watch the plan** unfold — notice it working across apps, not just answering.
4. When it offers to send/post/schedule anything, **read the preview before you approve**.
5. Keep going in the same thread: `Now turn that into a 3-bullet summary I can paste into Teams.`

## What good looks like
It took **several steps across more than one app** and handed you a small deliverable — not a one-line answer.

## Stretch it
Chain two tasks in one message: `Check my calendar for today, then draft prep notes for my first meeting.`

## Troubleshooting
- If it just answers like a chatbot, add **context and a source**: who it's for, which files or people, and the format you want back.
