# Challenge 4 — Let Cowork Drive the Browser

**Time:** ~8 min · **You'll need:** Microsoft **Edge** (Browser Use admin-enabled), `07_Receipts.csv`

## Goal
Watch Cowork act on the open web — and feel exactly where it hands control back to you for anything sensitive.

## Steps
1. Confirm **Browser Use** is enabled (Edge). If it isn't, watch a teammate or the recording.
2. Try a real, low-stakes web task:
   ```
   Using the receipts in 07_Receipts.csv, start an expense report on [expense site] and fill in the vendor, date, and amount for each line.
   ```
   or
   ```
   Go to [internal portal], pull this week's status into a short summary, and paste it back to me.
   ```
3. When it hits a **login, MFA, or CAPTCHA**, it hands you the tab — complete that step, then let it resume.
4. Review the final result before anything is submitted.

## What good looks like
Cowork drove Edge, paused for the sensitive step, and gave you back the wheel at the right moment.

## Stretch it
`Do the same thing but stop before submitting and show me a summary of what you filled in.`

## Troubleshooting
- No Browser Use? It's admin-gated — note it for your IT team (see [FAQ](../glossary-and-faq.md)).
- It's cautious by design — if it pauses, that's the safety hand-off working.
