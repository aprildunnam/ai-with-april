# Challenge 2 — The Morning Kickstart

**Time:** ~10 min · **You'll need:** your own inbox/calendar (optional: `01_ProjectAtlas_Brief.md`, `06_Inbox_Samples.txt`)

## Goal
Chain several tasks in **one** conversation — the way you'd brief a real coworker on a Monday.

## Steps
1. Open Cowork (model on **Auto**).
2. Start with the whole outcome, then let it work:

   ```
   It's Monday morning. Get me fully prepped for the week:
   1) brief me on today — meetings, key emails, and priorities
   2) prep me for my first meeting
   3) find and summarize a document relevant to my work this week
   4) draft a status update email to my manager
   ```
3. As it goes, **approve or edit** each action it wants to take.
4. Refine in-thread: `Make the status email shorter and add a "blockers" line.`

## What good looks like
All four done in **one thread, no app-switching**, ending with a draft you'd actually send.

## Stretch it
`Now schedule this same briefing to run every weekday at 8am.`

## Troubleshooting
- Too generic? Point it at specifics: `Use my Project Atlas emails and this week's calendar.`
