# Lab B — The Executive Update

**Time:** ~25 min · **Team:** 3–4 · **Files:** `03_ProjectAtlas_StatusData.csv`, `09_QuarterlyResults.csv`, `05_BrandStyleGuide.md`

## Scenario
Your VP needs a status update by end of day. Make it sharp, on-brand, and grounded in real numbers.

## Your mission
1. Upload the status data, quarterly results, and style guide.
2. Surface what matters:
   ```
   Read the Project Atlas status data and quarterly results. What are the 3 things a VP needs to know, and what's the single biggest risk?
   ```
3. Build the update to standard:
   ```
   Write a one-page executive update following our brand style guide: overall status, key metrics vs last quarter, risks with owners, and decisions needed. Keep the tone plain and direct.
   ```
4. If you have time:
   ```
   Turn that into a 3-slide summary I could drop into a leadership deck.
   ```

## Deliverable
A leadership-ready status summary (bonus: 2–3 slides).

## Share-out (3 min)
- How did the style guide change the output?
- What would you trust it to do unattended next time?

## Stretch
`Audit the update against the style guide and flag anything off-standard.`
