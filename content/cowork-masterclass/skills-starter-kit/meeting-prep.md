# Meeting Prep

## When to use
Trigger on: "prep me for my [time] meeting", "prep my next meeting", "get me ready for [meeting]".

## Steps
1. Find the meeting on my calendar and read the invite + agenda.
2. Pull attendee context (roles) and any related emails or documents from the last 2 weeks.
3. Produce the briefing below.

## Output format
**Meeting:** <title> · <time>
- **Purpose & desired outcome**
- **Who's there** (name — role — one line)
- **What you need to know** (3–5 bullets)
- **Likely questions / decisions**
- **Your talking points**

## Rules
- Keep it to one screen.
- Flag anything unresolved from the last related thread.
