About

Making Microsoft AI make sense.

I'm April Dunnam, Principal Cloud Advocate at Microsoft. I've spent 10+ years building on SharePoint, M365, and the Power Platform. Now I help people navigate where all of it is heading: AI agents, Copilot, and what actually works when you have to ship in the real world.

Why this hub exists

Most AI content is either too abstract to use on a Monday morning, or too narrow to be useful outside one specific tool. AI with April is my attempt at the middle: practical paths through AI, Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Studio, Cowork, and Power Platform, written for how people actually work — not a hype reel.

It's organized around three starting points, because "curious professional," "Microsoft maker," and "technical builder" need different entry points into the same material. Pick the one that's you on the homepage, or browse everything in the resource library.
